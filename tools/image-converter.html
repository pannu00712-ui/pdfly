Why convert image formats online?

Different devices, websites, and applications often require specific image formats. Converting an image lets you switch between common formats without recreating or editing the original file, making sharing and compatibility much easier.

PDFly converts images directly in your browser, keeping your files on your device throughout the process. There's no sign-up, no watermark, and no software installation required. It works on Windows, Mac, Linux, Android, and iPhone.

Best Practices & Limitations

Tips for best results

  • Use PNG for screenshots, logos, and anything with text or sharp edges; use JPEG for photographs.
  • WebP gives the smallest file size for web use if your target platform supports it.
  • Batch convert a whole folder at once when preparing images for a website or document set.

Limitations & when not to use this tool

  • Converting from a lossy format (JPEG) to another lossy format doesn't recover quality already lost.
  • Transparency is lost when converting to JPEG, since JPEG doesn't support an alpha channel.
  • Very large batches process one at a time in-browser, so extremely large sets may take a while.

Frequently asked questions

Which image formats are supported?

PDFly supports popular formats such as JPG, PNG, WebP, and other commonly used image types.

Will the image quality stay the same?

Quality is preserved whenever possible, although some formats use lossy compression that may slightly reduce image quality.

Can I convert multiple images at once?

Yes. Batch conversion is supported for compatible image formats.

Will transparent backgrounds be preserved?

Transparency is retained only in formats that support it, such as PNG and WebP.
