Why crop a PDF?

Scanned documents, printer exports, and design-software PDFs often carry extra white space, uneven margins, or dark scanner borders that make a document look untidy and waste paper when printed. Cropping hides that unwanted area without touching the underlying text or images.

Drag the crop boundaries in your browser, preview each page, and download — no upload, no watermark, and the original content stays intact inside the file.

Common uses

  • Remove scanner borders and shadows
  • Trim margins before printing
  • Clean up exported reports
  • Enlarge diagrams and handouts
  • Prepare cleaner client-facing files

Frequently asked questions

Does cropping delete PDF content?

No. Cropping changes the visible page area without modifying the underlying document content — hidden areas may still exist within the PDF's structure.

Can I crop all pages at once?

If the pages share the same layout, the same crop can generally be applied across the document. Pages with different layouts may need individual adjustments.

Will cropping reduce file size or image quality?

Not necessarily. Cropping changes what's visible, not the embedded content, so file size often stays similar and image/text quality is unaffected.

Are password-protected PDFs supported?

Protected PDFs with editing restrictions may need to be unlocked before cropping can be applied.
