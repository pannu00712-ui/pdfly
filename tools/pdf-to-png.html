Why convert PDF to PNG?

A full PDF isn't always what you need — sometimes it's just one slide, diagram, or page as a standalone image for a website, presentation, or design project. PNG preserves sharp text and fine detail, which makes it a better fit than a compressed format when clarity matters.

Because the conversion runs entirely in your browser, your PDF never touches a server — the source file and the images it becomes both stay on your device.

Frequently asked questions

What is the advantage of PNG over JPG?

PNG uses lossless compression, which helps preserve sharp text and graphics without the compression artifacts common in JPG images.

Will every PDF page become a separate PNG?

Yes. Each page is converted into its own PNG image for easier editing and sharing.

Can I convert scanned PDFs?

Yes. Scanned PDFs convert into PNG images just like standard PDF documents.

Will my original PDF be changed?

No. Your original PDF stays unchanged. PDFly creates separate PNG images while preserving the source file.
