Why compress images online?

Large image files can slow down websites, exceed email attachment limits, and take up unnecessary storage space. Compressing images reduces file size while maintaining good visual quality, making them easier to upload, share, and store.

PDFly compresses images directly in your browser, so your files never leave your device. There's no account to create, no watermark, and no software to install. It works on Windows, Mac, Linux, Android, and iPhone using any modern browser.

Best Practices & Limitations

Tips for best results

  • Use JPEG for photos and PNG only when you need transparency — JPEG compresses photographic content far more efficiently.
  • Compress before embedding images into a document rather than after, to keep the final PDF small from the start.
  • Batch-compress a folder of images at once instead of one at a time to save effort.

Limitations & when not to use this tool

  • Heavier compression reduces image quality — for print-quality output, keep compression light.
  • Already-compressed images (like re-saved JPEGs) won't shrink much further without visible quality loss.
  • Doesn't upscale or restore quality lost in a previous compression pass.

Frequently asked questions

Will image quality decrease?

Compression reduces file size while preserving as much quality as possible. Higher compression levels may result in some visible quality loss.

Which image formats are supported?

Common formats such as JPG, PNG, and WebP are supported, depending on the selected compression options.

Can I compress multiple images together?

Yes. You can process multiple supported images in one session to save time.

Does compression change image dimensions?

Not unless you choose a resize option. Standard compression only reduces file size.
