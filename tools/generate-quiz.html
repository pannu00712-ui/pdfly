Quiz

AI-generated quizzes are for study purposes only. Always verify against the original document.

Why generate a quiz from a PDF?

Turning notes or study materials into practice questions helps reinforce learning and identify knowledge gaps. Generating a quiz from a PDF makes revision more interactive without creating questions manually.

PDFly creates quizzes from supported PDF documents directly in your browser whenever possible. Your files stay on your device, with no sign-up, no watermark, and no software installation required. It works on Windows, Mac, Linux, Android, and iPhone.

Best Practices & Limitations

Tips for best results

  • Works best on text-heavy study material like notes, textbook chapters, or lecture transcripts.
  • Review generated questions before using them for a graded assessment — treat them as a study aid, not a final answer key.
  • Shorter, focused documents produce more relevant questions than very long mixed-topic PDFs.

Limitations & when not to use this tool

  • Question quality depends on how clearly structured the source text is — dense or poorly formatted PDFs produce weaker quizzes.
  • This tool sends content to a server for AI processing, unlike PDFly's local browser tools.
  • Not a substitute for human-reviewed assessments in formal educational settings.

Frequently asked questions

What types of questions are generated?

Depending on the document, quizzes may include multiple-choice, true/false, or short-answer questions.

Can I generate quizzes from scanned PDFs?

Yes, but OCR may be required before text can be analyzed accurately.

Are the answers included?

Yes. Generated quizzes can include answer keys when supported by the content.

Will every PDF produce a useful quiz?

Not always. Documents with little text, poor scan quality, or mostly images may produce limited or less accurate quiz results.
