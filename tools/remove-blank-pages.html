How to remove blank pages from a PDF

  1. Drop your PDF into the tool above, or click to browse your device.
  2. Adjust the sensitivity slider to control how aggressively the tool detects blank pages — lower values are more sensitive.
  3. Click "Remove Blank Pages" to analyze and clean your document. Everything runs in your browser — nothing is uploaded.

How PDFly detects blank pages

PDFly uses a sophisticated image-based detection method to identify truly empty pages. Here's how it works:

  • Page rendering: Each page is rendered as an image using pdf.js, the same engine used by Firefox and Chrome.
  • Pixel analysis: The tool scans every pixel on the page, counting how many pixels are not white (or near-white).
  • Threshold detection: If the number of non-white pixels falls below the sensitivity threshold, the page is marked as blank.
  • Automatic removal: Detected blank pages are removed, and the remaining pages are compiled into a clean PDF.

Understanding sensitivity settings

  • Lower sensitivity (1-10%): More aggressive. Detects pages with very minimal content as blank. Use this for documents that are mostly white with occasional small marks.
  • Medium sensitivity (11-25%): Balanced default. Works well for most documents — scanned files, reports, and presentations.
  • Higher sensitivity (26-50%): Less aggressive. Only removes pages that are completely empty. Use this if the tool is incorrectly removing pages with small watermarks or logos.

When to remove blank pages

  • Scanned documents: Scanners often insert blank pages between double-sided scans.
  • Exported presentations: PowerPoint exports can include trailing blank slides.
  • PDF conversions: Converting from Word or other formats sometimes adds blank pages.
  • Email attachments: Reduce file size by removing empty pages before sending.

Before you remove blank pages

  • Check preview: Review the detected blank pages preview to ensure no important pages are marked for removal.
  • Adjust sensitivity: If pages with logos or watermarks are being detected as blank, increase the sensitivity value.
  • Keep a backup: Always keep the original file in case you need to restore removed pages.

Common mistakes

  • Setting sensitivity too low, causing pages with small logos or page numbers to be removed incorrectly.
  • Setting sensitivity too high, missing blank pages that should be removed.
  • Deleting the original before checking that the cleaned PDF opens correctly.
  • Assuming all white pages are blank — some pages may have white text or images that are invisible to the eye but present in the PDF.

Troubleshooting

  • The tool detected too many pages as blank: Increase the sensitivity value (move the slider to the right). This makes the detection less aggressive.
  • The tool missed some blank pages: Decrease the sensitivity value (move the slider to the left). This makes the detection more sensitive.
  • A page with a faint watermark was removed: Increase sensitivity — faint marks should be enough to prevent removal with the right threshold.
  • The cleaned PDF is missing content: Check the preview before clicking Remove. If content was removed, adjust sensitivity and re-run.
