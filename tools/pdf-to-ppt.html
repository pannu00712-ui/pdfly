Why convert PDF to PowerPoint?

Presentations saved as PDFs are easy to share, but they're difficult to edit when you need to update text, replace images, or reuse slides. Converting to PowerPoint helps you make changes without rebuilding the presentation from the beginning, saving time on revisions and updates.

PDFly converts PDF slides into editable PowerPoint (.pptx) files directly in your browser — no sign-up, no watermark, no software to install.

Frequently asked questions

Will my slide layout be preserved?

Most text, images, and slide formatting are retained. Complex graphics, animations, or custom fonts may need small adjustments after conversion.

Can I convert scanned presentation PDFs?

Scanned PDFs are image-based and require OCR for editable text. Without OCR, slide content may remain as images.

Which PowerPoint format will I receive?

The converted presentation is provided as a standard .pptx file, compatible with Microsoft PowerPoint, Google Slides, LibreOffice Impress, and other presentation software.

Why do some elements move after conversion?

PDF files don't store editable presentation objects. Highly customized layouts, embedded fonts, or layered graphics can result in minor positioning differences that may require manual editing.
