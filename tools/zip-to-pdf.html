Why convert a ZIP archive to PDF?

If you've received PDF documents inside a ZIP archive, extracting them manually can be an extra step. ZIP to PDF makes it easier to access bundled PDF files quickly from a compressed archive.

PDFly extracts supported PDF files from ZIP archives directly in your browser. Your files remain on your device throughout the process, with no sign-up, no watermark, and no software installation required. It works on Windows, Mac, Linux, Android, and iPhone.

Frequently asked questions

Does this merge PDFs together?

No. Each PDF is extracted separately unless you choose to merge them using another tool.

Can password-protected ZIP files be opened?

Password-protected ZIP archives may require the correct password before extraction.

What happens to non-PDF files?

Unsupported file types are ignored or excluded from PDF extraction.

Will the extracted PDFs change?

No. The original PDF files remain unchanged after extraction.
