Why convert PDF to EPUB?

PDF pages are fixed — the layout that looks right on a laptop screen often means constant pinch-zooming and side-scrolling on a phone or e-reader. EPUB fixes that by reflowing text to fit whatever screen it's displayed on, with adjustable font size and proper page-turning, the way eBooks are meant to be read.

This matters most for text-heavy PDFs — reports, manuscripts, or long documents — that you want to read comfortably on a Kindle, Kobo, tablet, or phone instead of a computer screen. Since conversion happens locally in your browser, the file never leaves your device.

Frequently asked questions

Will complex layouts (tables, columns) convert cleanly?

Plain text and simple layouts convert best. Heavily designed PDFs — multi-column magazines, forms, or pages with precise graphic layout — may lose some formatting once reflowed, since that's how EPUB works by design.

Can I set the book title and author before converting?

Yes — the EPUB settings let you set the book title, author, and output filename, which shows up correctly in Kindle, Kobo, and other e-reader libraries.

Will scanned PDFs (images only) work?

Scanned pages without a text layer will carry over as images rather than reflowable text. Run the PDF through OCR first if you need the text itself to reflow and be selectable.

Do I need to install anything?

No. Everything runs in your browser tab — no software, no account, no sign-up.
