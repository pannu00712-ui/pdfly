Why convert PDF to text?

PDFs are great for preserving layout, but that's exactly what makes the text inside them hard to reuse. Copying directly out of a PDF often brings broken line breaks and odd spacing along with it. Converting to plain text strips that away, leaving content that's easy to edit, search, translate, or drop into another document.

It's useful for research, editing, documentation, data processing, and archiving — anywhere you need the words without the formatting.

How it works

Upload your PDF, let PDFly extract the readable text, review the results, then copy it or download it as a .txt file. Your original PDF stays exactly as it was — a separate text version is created alongside it.

Limitations & tips

Extraction works best on PDFs that already contain selectable text. Scanned pages are just images, so they need OCR first before any text can be pulled out. Complex layouts — tables, multiple columns, headers and footers — may not come out in the same order they appear visually, and password-protected files may need to be unlocked first.

For the cleanest result, use text-based PDFs where possible, and review the output afterward for paragraph breaks and special characters.

Frequently asked questions

Which PDFs work best for text extraction?

PDFs with selectable text generally produce the best results. Scanned documents usually require OCR before the text can be extracted accurately.

Will the formatting stay the same?

No. Plain text removes most formatting, including fonts, images, page layouts, and graphics, while preserving the readable text whenever possible.

Can I extract text from scanned PDFs?

Yes, but scanned PDFs typically need OCR before the text can be recognized and converted into editable content.

Will my original PDF be modified?

No. Your original document remains unchanged. PDFly creates a separate text version while preserving the source PDF.

Can I convert password-protected PDFs?

PDFs with reading restrictions may need to be unlocked before text extraction is possible.

Can I use PDF to Text on my phone?

Yes. The tool works in modern mobile browsers on Android, iPhone, and tablets without requiring an app.
