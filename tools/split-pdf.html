Why split a PDF?

Many PDF files contain sections that don't need to be shared or stored together. You might need to send only a contract signature page, separate invoices from a monthly report, or extract specific chapters from a large document. Splitting saves time and avoids creating unnecessary copies by hand.

PDFly splits PDF files directly in your browser, so your documents stay on your device throughout the process. Choose the pages or page ranges you need and download the new files in seconds.

Common uses

  • Extract selected pages
  • Split by page range
  • Separate document chapters
  • Share specific sections
  • Organize large PDF files

Frequently asked questions

Can I split a PDF into multiple files at once?

Yes. You can split by individual pages or page ranges, depending on how you want to organize the document.

Will the formatting change after splitting?

No. Each output PDF keeps the original page layout, fonts, and image quality because only the selected pages are separated.

Can I split scanned PDF files?

Yes, but scanned pages remain image-based. If you need editable text, OCR is required before or after splitting.

Why won't my PDF split?

Password-protected or DRM-restricted PDFs may need to be unlocked first. Corrupted files can also prevent successful processing.
