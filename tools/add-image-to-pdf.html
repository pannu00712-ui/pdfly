Why add an image to a PDF?

Adding an image is one of the easiest ways to personalize or update a PDF without recreating it from scratch. A company logo on an invoice, a signature on a contract, a stamp on a certificate, a QR code on a flyer — all of these are quicker to drop onto an existing PDF than to rebuild the document.

Drop your PDF and image above, drag the image where you want it, resize it, adjust opacity for watermark-style placement, and download. Since everything runs in your browser, neither file is uploaded anywhere.

Common uses

  • Company logos on invoices and reports
  • Handwritten or digital signatures on contracts
  • Official stamps or seals
  • Product photos in catalogs
  • QR codes for payments or links
  • Watermarks for branding or copyright

Frequently asked questions

Which image formats are supported?

PNG, JPG, JPEG, and WebP. Transparent PNGs work best for logos and signatures since they blend naturally with the page.

Can I add more than one image?

Yes, repeat the process for each image you want to place on the document.

Will this reduce my PDF's quality?

No, the original document quality is preserved — only the inserted image is added on top.

Do I need to install anything?

No. Everything runs in your browser tab — no software, no account, no sign-up.
