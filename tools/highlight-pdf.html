Why highlight text in a PDF?

Reviewing long documents is easier when important text stands out. Highlighting key sections helps you mark notes, identify deadlines, emphasize important clauses, or prepare documents for collaboration without changing the original content.

PDFly lets you highlight text in PDF files directly in your browser. Your documents remain on your device throughout the process, with no account required, no watermark, and no software installation. It works on Windows, Mac, Linux, Android, and iPhone.

Frequently asked questions

Can I change the highlight color?

Yes. Supported highlight colors can be selected before saving your edited PDF.

Can I highlight scanned PDFs?

Scanned PDFs may require OCR before text can be highlighted accurately. Otherwise, highlighting may work only as an annotation.

Will highlights be visible in other PDF readers?

Yes. Standard PDF highlights are supported by most modern PDF viewing applications.

Can I remove highlights later?

Yes. As long as the PDF remains editable, highlights can usually be modified or removed before flattening the document.
