Why use an online PDF reader?

Not every computer or phone has a dedicated PDF viewer installed, and adding one just to open a single document isn't always practical. A browser-based reader gives you instant access to contracts, ebooks, manuals, and reports without changing the file or installing anything.

Because the reader opens PDFs directly in your browser, your document stays on your device the entire time you're viewing it.

Best Practices & Limitations

Tips for best results

  • Use the search function to jump straight to a keyword instead of scrolling through long documents.
  • Bookmark or note page numbers for sections you'll need to reference again later.
  • Switch to dark mode for comfortable reading of long documents at night.

Limitations & when not to use this tool

  • Very large files (500+ pages or huge embedded images) may take a moment to fully load.
  • Interactive form fields display correctly but advanced scripted forms may behave differently than in Adobe Acrobat.
  • Doesn't edit the file — for changes, use PDF Editor or the relevant PDFly tool.

Frequently asked questions

Can I open any PDF file?

Most standard PDF documents can be opened. Password-protected PDFs need the correct password before they can be viewed.

Can I edit my PDF in the PDF Reader?

No. The PDF Reader is for viewing documents only. Use PDFly's PDF Editor if you need to make changes.

Does the PDF Reader support scanned PDFs?

Yes. Scanned PDFs display normally, though image-based pages won't have selectable text unless OCR has already been applied.

Is my PDF uploaded to a server?

No. PDFly opens PDFs directly in your browser, so your file stays on your device the whole time you're viewing it.
