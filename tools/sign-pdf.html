PDF Signed!

Why sign a PDF online?

Printing, signing by hand, and scanning documents again can slow down simple tasks. Adding an electronic signature to a PDF lets you complete agreements, forms, and approvals much faster while keeping the document ready to share.

PDFly lets you sign PDF files directly in your browser. Your documents never leave your device — no account, no watermark, no software installation.

Common uses

  • Sign contracts online
  • Approve business documents
  • Complete application forms
  • Add handwritten signatures
  • Finalize agreements

Best Practices & Limitations

Tips for best results

  • Use a consistent signature style across documents so it's recognizable as genuinely yours.
  • Position the signature clearly within the designated signature block to avoid ambiguity.
  • Keep a signed copy for your own records immediately after signing, before sending it onward.

Limitations & when not to use this tool

  • This adds a visual signature image/drawing to the page — it isn't a certificate-based digital signature with cryptographic verification.
  • For contracts requiring legally binding e-signatures with an audit trail, a dedicated e-signature service may be required by your jurisdiction or counterparty.
  • Doesn't verify the signer's identity — it simply places the signature you provide onto the document.

Frequently asked questions

Can I draw my own signature?

Yes. You can draw or insert your signature and place it anywhere on the PDF.

Is my signature legally valid?

Legal validity depends on your local laws and the document requirements. PDFly provides an electronic signing tool but doesn't certify legal compliance.

Can I edit the signature later?

Before saving, you can reposition or replace your signature. After downloading, you'll need to edit the PDF again to make changes.

Does signing reduce PDF quality?

No. Adding a signature doesn't affect the quality of existing pages or images.
