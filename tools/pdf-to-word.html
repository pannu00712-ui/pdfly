Why convert PDF to Word?

Sometimes you need to update a contract, edit a report, or reuse text from a PDF without starting from scratch. Recreating the document manually takes time, especially when the original contains tables, headings, or formatted paragraphs. Converting to Word makes editing much easier while preserving as much of the original layout as possible.

PDFly converts PDFs into editable Word (.docx) documents directly in your browser, so your files never leave your device — no account, no watermark on the output.

Common uses

  • Edit PDF text
  • Update business documents
  • Reuse report content
  • Extract formatted text
  • Revise contracts quickly

Best Practices & Limitations

Tips for best results

  • Works best on text-heavy documents with standard formatting — reports, letters, contracts.
  • Review headers, footers, and page breaks after conversion, since these sometimes shift slightly.
  • For a document you'll heavily edit afterward, this is faster than retyping from scratch.

Limitations & when not to use this tool

  • Complex multi-column layouts, forms, and heavily designed pages may not convert with pixel-perfect formatting.
  • Scanned PDFs need OCR PDF run first — this tool converts the existing text layer, not images of text.
  • Embedded fonts not available on your system may be substituted with a similar-looking alternative in Word.

Frequently asked questions

Will my formatting stay the same?

Most text, images, and basic formatting are preserved. Complex layouts, unusual fonts, or heavily designed documents may need minor adjustments after conversion.

Can I convert scanned PDFs?

Scanned PDFs are image-based. They require OCR to create editable text, so results depend on the quality of the original scan.

Does this support password-protected PDFs?

Protected PDFs must be unlocked before conversion. DRM-restricted files may not be supported.

What Word format will I receive?

The converted file is provided as a standard .docx document, compatible with Microsoft Word, Google Docs, LibreOffice, and other modern word processors.
