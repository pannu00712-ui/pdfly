How to rewrite a PDF with AI

  1. Drop your PDF into the tool above, or click to browse your device.
  2. Choose your preferred tone (Professional, Simple, Formal, or Casual) and desired length.
  3. Click "Rewrite with AI" and review the rewritten text. Copy or download the result as a .txt file.

How PDFly's AI rewriting works

PDFly uses advanced AI language models to understand and rephrase your PDF content. Here's what happens behind the scenes:

  • Text extraction: Using pdf.js, the tool extracts all readable text from your PDF, preserving paragraph structure.
  • AI processing: The extracted text is sent to our AI API, which rewrites the content according to your selected tone and length preferences.
  • Result delivery: The rewritten text is displayed instantly in your browser — you can copy it or download it as a .txt file.

Privacy note: Your text is sent to our AI API for processing. We do not store or share your content. For complete privacy, consider using offline alternatives for sensitive documents.

Understanding tone and length options

  • Professional: Polished, business-appropriate language. Ideal for work documents, reports, and formal communication.
  • Simple: Clear, straightforward language. Perfect for making complex content more accessible.
  • Formal: Academic or official tone. Suitable for legal documents, academic papers, and official correspondence.
  • Casual: Relaxed, conversational language. Great for blog posts, emails, and informal content.
  • Length options: Choose "Same length" for a direct paraphrase, "Shorter" for concise summaries, or "Longer" for expanded explanations.

When to use AI rewriting

  • Improving clarity: Rewrite complex or poorly written text to make it easier to understand.
  • Adjusting tone: Convert informal notes into professional reports, or formal documents into casual language.
  • Summarizing content: Use the "Shorter" option to create concise summaries of lengthy documents.
  • Rephrasing for originality: Paraphrase content to avoid plagiarism while maintaining the core message.
  • Readability enhancement: Make text more accessible for different audiences.

Before you rewrite

  • Check the source: Ensure you have permission to rewrite the content.
  • Verify extraction: Scanned PDFs or image-based documents may not extract text properly. Use OCR PDF first for scanned documents.
  • Review the output: AI rewriting can sometimes change meaning — always review the result before using it.

Common mistakes

  • Using the tool on scanned PDFs without OCR — no text will be extracted.
  • Expecting the AI to preserve exact technical terminology — specialized terms may be simplified.
  • Not reviewing the output — AI can sometimes produce unintended meaning changes.
  • Relying on AI rewriting for sensitive or confidential documents without considering privacy implications.

Troubleshooting

  • No text was extracted from my PDF: Your PDF may be scanned or image-based. Use OCR PDF to convert it to searchable text first.
  • The rewrite doesn't make sense: Try a different tone or length setting. Some content (like lists or data) doesn't rewrite well — consider rewriting paragraphs instead.
  • The tool is slow: Large documents take longer to process. The tool limits text to 40,000 characters for performance.
  • I got a network error: Check your internet connection — the tool needs to communicate with the AI API.
