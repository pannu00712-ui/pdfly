Why convert Word to PDF?

Word documents can look different depending on the device, software version, or installed fonts. Converting to PDF preserves the layout, making it easier to share resumes, reports, invoices, proposals, and other documents without unexpected formatting changes.

PDFly converts .docx files to PDF directly in your browser, so your documents stay on your device throughout — no sign-up, no watermark, nothing to install.

Common uses

  • Share fixed layouts
  • Convert resumes to PDF
  • Save business reports
  • Submit official forms

Frequently asked questions

Which Word file formats are supported?

The tool is designed for .docx files. Older .doc files may need to be opened and saved as .docx before conversion.

Will the PDF match my Word document?

Most fonts, images, tables, and page layouts are preserved. If your document uses unsupported fonts or advanced features, small formatting differences may occur.

Can I convert large Word documents?

Yes, although very large files with many high-resolution images may take a little longer to process depending on your device.

Does the PDF remain editable?

No. A PDF is intended for consistent viewing and sharing. To make changes later, edit the original Word document and convert it again.
