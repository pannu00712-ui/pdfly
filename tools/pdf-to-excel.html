Why convert PDF to Excel?

Tables inside PDF files are often difficult to edit or analyze. Copying rows and columns by hand can break formatting and waste time, especially with invoices, financial reports, budgets, or spreadsheets saved as PDFs. Converting to Excel makes it easier to sort, calculate, filter, and update data.

PDFly extracts tables from PDF files into editable Excel spreadsheets directly in your browser — no sign-up, no watermark, no software to install.

Common uses

  • Extract financial tables
  • Convert invoice data
  • Edit spreadsheet content
  • Analyze report figures
  • Organize business records

Best Practices & Limitations

Tips for best results

  • Works best on PDFs with clear table structures and gridlines rather than plain-text data.
  • Check merged cells and multi-row headers after conversion — these sometimes need manual adjustment.
  • For financial reports with multiple tables per page, verify each table landed in the correct sheet.

Limitations & when not to use this tool

  • Tables without visible borders or ones formatted with just spacing can be harder to detect accurately.
  • Formulas aren't recovered — only the calculated values shown in the PDF are extracted.
  • Scanned (image-based) PDFs need OCR PDF run first, since this tool reads the text layer.

Frequently asked questions

Will all tables be converted accurately?

Most standard tables convert well. Complex layouts, merged cells, or unusual formatting may require minor adjustments after conversion.

Can I convert scanned PDF tables?

Scanned PDFs require OCR before table data can become editable. The quality of the scan affects the final results.

What Excel format will I receive?

The converted file is provided in a standard Excel-compatible format that works with Microsoft Excel, Google Sheets, LibreOffice Calc, and similar spreadsheet applications.

Why are some cells or columns misplaced?

PDFs aren't designed to store spreadsheet structures. Documents with irregular table layouts or overlapping content may need a small amount of manual cleanup after conversion.
