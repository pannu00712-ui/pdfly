Why translate a PDF?

Many important documents are shared as PDFs, which makes them awkward to edit or translate manually — copying text page by page into a translation tool is slow and tends to mangle the formatting.

PDFly extracts the readable text and translates it into another language in one pass, which is useful for international business, academic research, travel documents, technical manuals, contracts, and multilingual communication generally.

Good for

  • Business reports and contracts
  • Research papers and articles
  • User manuals and technical documentation
  • Study materials and ebooks
  • Travel documents

Limitations & tips

Translation quality depends on how readable the original PDF is — documents with selectable text produce the best results, while scanned PDFs need OCR first. Complex layouts, tables, images, charts, and specialized terminology may need a manual review after translation, and password-protected PDFs may need to be unlocked before processing.

For legal, medical, financial, or otherwise high-stakes documents, treat the output as a strong first draft and have the specialized terminology verified.
