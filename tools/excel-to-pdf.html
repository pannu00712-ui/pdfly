Why convert Excel to PDF?

Excel spreadsheets are useful for calculations, but they can appear differently depending on the software, printer settings, or screen size. Converting to PDF locks the layout, making reports, invoices, budgets, and data sheets easier to share without worrying about formatting changes.

PDFly converts Excel spreadsheets into PDF documents directly in your browser, so your files never leave your device — no account, no watermark, nothing to install.

Frequently asked questions

Which Excel file formats are supported?

The tool supports modern Excel spreadsheet formats such as .xlsx. Older .xls files may need to be converted to .xlsx first for the best results.

Will formulas still work in the PDF?

No. A PDF displays the calculated results but doesn't keep formulas or editable spreadsheet functions.

Will my page layout stay the same?

PDFly preserves your worksheet layout as closely as possible. Very wide spreadsheets or custom print settings may affect page breaks and scaling.

Can I convert spreadsheets with multiple sheets?

Yes. Multiple worksheets can be included in the generated PDF, depending on the structure of the uploaded Excel file.
