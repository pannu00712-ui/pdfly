Why compare two PDFs?

A colleague sends back a revised contract, or you download a new version of a report and need to know exactly what changed. Reading both documents side by side is slow, and small edits are easy to miss.

PDFly compares the text in both files directly in your browser and highlights what was added, removed, or changed — so you can review the differences instead of every page.

Common uses

  • Compare contract revisions
  • Verify requested edits were made
  • Check manuscript or report versions
  • Review updated research papers
  • Confirm policy or handbook changes

Frequently asked questions

Does it compare layout or just text?

This tool compares the readable text content word by word. Visual/layout-only changes, like font, color, or spacing, aren't detected if the underlying text is unchanged.

Can I compare scanned PDFs?

Only if they already have a text layer. Run scanned files through OCR PDF first so their text can be extracted and compared.

Will my documents be uploaded anywhere?

No. The comparison runs directly in your browser whenever possible, so your files stay on your device.

Are password-protected PDFs supported?

An encrypted or permission-restricted PDF may need to be unlocked first before it can be compared.
