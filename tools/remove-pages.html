How to remove pages from a PDF

  1. Drop your PDF into the tool above, or click to browse your device.
  2. Enter the page numbers you want to remove — use single numbers (e.g., 3), ranges (e.g., 5-10), or a mix (e.g., 1, 3-5, 8).
  3. Click "Remove Pages & Download" to get your trimmed PDF. Everything runs in your browser — nothing is uploaded.

How PDFly removes pages

PDFly uses the pdf-lib library to copy only the pages you want to keep into a fresh PDF. The original file's content, formatting, fonts, and images are preserved exactly — only the pages you specify are excluded.

This is a lossless operation. The removed pages are simply omitted from the new file, and the remaining pages retain all their original quality and metadata.

Page number formats you can use

  • Single page: 3 — removes page 3
  • Range: 5-10 — removes pages 5, 6, 7, 8, 9, and 10
  • Multiple: 1, 3, 5-7, 12 — removes pages 1, 3, 5, 6, 7, and 12
  • All except: To keep only specific pages, remove all others. For example, to keep pages 2-4, remove 1, 5- (page 5 to the end).

When to remove pages from a PDF

  • Extra blank pages: Scans or documents often include blank pages that waste space.
  • Duplicates: Remove repeated pages that were accidentally included.
  • Unwanted sections: Extract only the chapters or sections you need from a larger document.
  • Sensitive information: Remove pages containing confidential or unnecessary data before sharing.
  • File size reduction: Fewer pages = smaller file, which is especially useful for email attachments.

Before you remove pages

  • Keep a backup: Always keep the original file in case you realize you removed the wrong pages.
  • Count carefully: Double-check your page numbers before clicking Remove. PDF page numbering starts at 1 (the first page).
  • Unlock first: If the PDF is password-protected, use Unlock PDF first.

Common mistakes

  • Entering page numbers that don't exist (e.g., page 20 in a 10-page document).
  • Using spaces incorrectly — 1- 5 doesn't work; use 1-5.
  • Accidentally removing all pages — the tool prevents this with a validation check.
  • Deleting the original before checking that the trimmed PDF opens correctly.

Troubleshooting

  • Nothing happens when I click Remove: Make sure you've entered page numbers in the correct format. The tool shows a preview of pages to remove as you type.
  • The output PDF is missing content: Check that you didn't accidentally remove pages you wanted to keep. The "Pages to keep" counter shows how many pages will remain.
  • The PDF won't open after processing: Some PDFs use features that pdf-lib can't fully preserve. Try Repair PDF on the original first, then remove pages.
  • I removed the wrong pages: Upload the original file again and re-enter the correct page numbers.
