Why convert JPG to PDF?

Photos are easy to share, but they aren't always the best format for printing, submitting, or storing documents. If you have scanned pages, receipts, certificates, or multiple images that belong together, combining them into a single PDF keeps everything organized and easier to manage.

PDFly converts one or more JPG images into a PDF directly in your browser — no account, no watermark, and your files stay on your device throughout.

Common uses

  • Combine scanned documents
  • Create photo PDFs
  • Save receipts digitally
  • Merge certificate images
  • Build printable documents

Best Practices & Limitations

Tips for best results

  • Arrange images in the intended reading order before converting — the order in the tool becomes the page order.
  • Combine multiple photos of a document (front and back of a receipt, both sides of an ID) into a single PDF this way.
  • Compress the resulting PDF afterward if you're combining many high-resolution photos.

Limitations & when not to use this tool

  • Each image becomes one full page — there's no automatic multi-image-per-page layout.
  • Image quality in the PDF can't exceed the quality of the source JPGs.
  • Not OCR'd automatically — run OCR PDF afterward if you need the text inside the images to be searchable.

Frequently asked questions

Can I combine multiple JPG files into one PDF?

Yes. You can upload multiple JPG images and merge them into a single PDF in your preferred order.

Will my image quality change?

The tool aims to preserve image quality. The final PDF quality also depends on the resolution of the original JPG files.

Can I rearrange pages before creating the PDF?

Yes. If multiple images are uploaded, you can organize their order before generating the PDF.

Does this support PNG or other image formats?

This tool is designed specifically for JPG images. For PNG files, use the dedicated PNG to PDF tool for the best compatibility.
