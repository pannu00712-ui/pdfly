Why convert text to PDF?

Plain text files are simple to edit, but they don't preserve formatting when shared across different devices. Converting text to PDF creates a consistent document that's easier to print, archive, and distribute without accidental changes.

PDFly converts text files into PDF directly in your browser. Your content never leaves your device, and there's no sign-up, no watermark, or software installation required.

Frequently asked questions

Which text formats are supported?

Standard plain text (.txt) files and pasted text are supported.

Will my formatting be preserved?

Line breaks and spacing are maintained, but advanced formatting such as colors or fonts isn't available in plain text files.

Can I use accented or non-English characters?

Common Latin-based characters (accents, European scripts) work fine. Characters outside that range — like Arabic, Chinese, or emoji — aren't supported by the PDF font used here; the tool will warn you if any get substituted.

Is there a file size limit?

Very large text files may take longer to process depending on your device and browser.
