Why convert PDFs to a ZIP archive?

Sharing multiple PDF files individually can be inconvenient. Converting PDFs into a ZIP archive keeps everything together in a single downloadable file, making storage, sharing, and organization much easier.

PDFly packages PDF files into a ZIP archive directly in your browser. Your files stay on your device, with no account required, no watermark, and no software installation. It works on Windows, Mac, Linux, Android, and iPhone.

Frequently asked questions

Does ZIP compression reduce PDF quality?

No. ZIP archives package files without changing the PDF content or quality.

Can I add multiple PDFs?

Yes. Multiple PDF files can be included in a single ZIP archive.

Will the PDFs be merged?

No. Each PDF remains a separate file inside the ZIP archive.

Can I open the ZIP on any device?

Yes. ZIP files are supported by most modern operating systems and archive utilities.
