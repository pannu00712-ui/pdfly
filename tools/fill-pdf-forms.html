Why fill PDF forms online?

Many PDF forms are designed to be completed digitally, but filling them manually or printing them first can be slow and inconvenient. Filling PDF forms electronically makes it easier to complete applications, contracts, registrations, and official documents while keeping the formatting intact.

PDFly lets you fill PDF forms directly in your browser, so your documents stay on your device throughout the process. There's no account to create, no watermark, and no software installation required. It works on Windows, Mac, Linux, Android, and iPhone using any modern browser.

Best Practices & Limitations

Tips for best results

  • Tab through fields in order to make sure none are accidentally skipped before downloading.
  • Save a blank copy of the original form before filling, in case you need to fill it again differently later.
  • Use Flatten PDF afterward if you want to lock in the values so they can't be edited by whoever receives the file.

Limitations & when not to use this tool

  • Only works with PDFs that already have interactive form fields — a scanned paper form needs OCR PDF or manual field creation first.
  • Digital signatures inside form fields aren't verified for legal validity by this tool — check your jurisdiction's requirements separately.
  • Complex forms with conditional logic or scripts may not render identically to how they'd behave in Adobe Acrobat.

Frequently asked questions

Can I edit existing PDF form fields?

Yes. You can fill supported interactive form fields such as text boxes, checkboxes, radio buttons, and dropdown menus.

What if my PDF isn't fillable?

If the document is a scanned PDF or contains no interactive fields, you'll need to add text manually or use OCR before editing.

Will my completed form stay editable?

Yes, unless you choose to flatten the PDF after filling it. Flattening makes the entries permanent.

Can I save a partially completed form?

Yes. You can download the updated PDF and continue editing later if the form remains editable.
