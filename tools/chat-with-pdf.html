Best Practices & Limitations

Tips for best results

  • Ask specific questions ('What's the termination clause?') rather than open-ended ones for sharper answers.
  • For long documents, ask for a section-by-section summary first, then drill into the parts that matter.
  • Cross-check any numbers or dates the AI gives you against the source page before relying on them.

Limitations & when not to use this tool

  • Answers are generated by an AI model and can occasionally misread scanned or poorly formatted PDFs — always verify anything critical.
  • This tool sends document content to a server for AI processing, unlike PDFly's browser-only tools — see the privacy note above.
  • Not a substitute for legal, medical, or financial review of the actual document.

Why chat with a PDF?

Reading long PDFs to find one answer can take time. Chat with PDF lets you ask questions about your document and quickly locate important information, summaries, or specific sections without manually searching every page.
