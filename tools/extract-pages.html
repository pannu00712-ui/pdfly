Why extract PDF pages?

Not every page in a PDF is always needed. You may want to save a single invoice, keep one chapter from a report, or send only the pages someone needs. Extracting pages creates a new PDF containing only the selected pages without changing the original document.

PDFly extracts PDF pages directly in your browser, so your files stay on your device throughout — no sign-up, no watermark, no software to install.

Common uses

  • Save selected pages
  • Share specific sections
  • Extract report chapters
  • Separate invoice pages
  • Create smaller PDFs

Best Practices & Limitations

Tips for best results

  • Use a page range (e.g. 3-7) for a contiguous chunk, or individual page numbers for scattered selections.
  • Extract first, then compress if the resulting file is still larger than expected.
  • Double-check page numbers against the PDF viewer's count, not the printed page numbers, since they can differ.

Limitations & when not to use this tool

  • Extracted pages keep their original formatting but lose any document-level bookmarks that pointed outside the selection.
  • Not meant for splitting into many separate files at once — use Split PDF for that.
  • Form field values on extracted pages are preserved but any related fields on non-extracted pages are lost.

Frequently asked questions

Can I extract multiple pages at once?

Yes. Select individual pages or a page range to create a new PDF containing only those pages.

Will the extracted pages keep their quality?

Yes. The original page layout, text, images, and formatting are preserved.

Does extracting pages modify my original PDF?

No. Your original file remains unchanged, and a separate PDF is created with the selected pages.

Can I extract pages from a protected PDF?

Password-protected or DRM-restricted PDFs may need to be unlocked before pages can be extracted.
