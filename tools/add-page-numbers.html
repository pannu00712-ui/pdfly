Why add page numbers to a PDF?

Large PDFs without page numbers get confusing fast — especially once they're printed or shared, when there's no way to say "check page 12" or cite a specific section. Numbering fixes that with almost no effort.

Pick a position, a numbering style, and a starting number, and PDFly stamps every page in your browser — no upload, no software, no watermark left behind.

Common uses

  • Business reports and proposals
  • Academic theses and dissertations
  • Contracts and legal documents
  • eBooks and manuals
  • Printed handouts and training material

Frequently asked questions

Can I choose where the numbers appear?

Yes — top or bottom, and left, center, or right. Bottom-center and bottom-right are the most common choices for reports and business documents.

Can I start numbering from something other than 1?

Yes. This is useful when a cover page or introduction should stay unnumbered and the count should start later in the document.

Will this overlap my existing headers or footers?

It can if the position you choose is already in use. If your PDF has its own header/footer content, pick an unused corner for the cleanest result.

Do I need to install anything?

No. Everything runs in your browser tab — no software, no account, no sign-up.
