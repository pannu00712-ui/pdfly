What you need, organized

One toolbox, six ways to use it

Every tool runs the same way — instantly, privately, in your browser.

Everything happens in your browser

Most "free" PDF tools quietly upload your file to a server. PDFly processes documents locally on your device using WebAssembly — your contracts, IDs, and drafts never leave your machine.

  • No file upload to a remote server
  • Works offline once the page is loaded
  • Nothing to delete later — there's nothing stored

Processing locally

No network request sent

Median tool completion time: under 2 seconds

Built for speed

No upload queue, no server round-trip, no waiting behind other people's files. Because processing happens on your own hardware, most operations finish before a typical page finishes loading.

  • No queueing behind other users
  • No file-size throttling
  • Batch multiple files without slowdown

AI that reads your documents, not your data

Ask questions, get a plain-language summary, or translate a contract in seconds. Our AI tools process only the specific request you make — nothing is stored or used to train models.
