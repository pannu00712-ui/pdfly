PDF Tools for Business

Sign, protect, redact, and prepare business documents without a subscription — free tools built for everyday paperwork.

Most day-to-day business paperwork — contracts, invoices, reports, NDAs, proposals — ends up as a PDF at some point, and handling it usually means one of a small set of recurring tasks: getting a signature, keeping a file confidential, combining several documents into one packet, or getting a file small enough to email. PDFly covers each of these without requiring a subscription, a company-wide license, or an account for every team member who needs to touch a file occasionally.

Sign PDF lets you add your own signature to a contract or agreement directly in the browser — useful for the common case of signing something yourself rather than collecting signatures from other people. Protect PDF adds password encryption to sensitive files like financial statements or HR documents before they're emailed or shared, while Redact PDF permanently removes specific sensitive details — account numbers, names, figures — from a document before it goes external. For documents you receive locked and need to work with, Unlock PDF removes a password you already know or have permission to remove.

Preparing documents for distribution is just as common: Merge PDF combines multiple reports, receipts, or contract pages into a single file for a client or auditor, while Compress PDF gets a large file under an email attachment limit without a visible quality loss. Add Watermark marks draft proposals or confidential material clearly before they're shared outside the company, and the PDF Metadata Editor lets you check or clean up the author, title, and other properties embedded in a file — worth doing before sending something externally under a company name.

Privacy matters more for business documents than almost any other category, since they often contain financial details, personal data, or confidential terms. Most PDFly tools process files entirely in your browser, which means the document itself is not uploaded to a server for tasks like merging, compressing, protecting, or signing. There's also no account required and no per-seat pricing, so a tool can be used by anyone on a team without procurement or IT sign-off.
