AI PDF Tools

Chat with, summarize, translate, explain, or quiz yourself on any PDF — powered by AI, free, and built to keep your files on your own device.

PDF files are easy to create but often hard to work with once you actually need to use what's inside them. A 40-page contract, a dense research paper, or a textbook chapter in a foreign language all share the same problem: reading and understanding them by hand takes time. AI PDF tools solve this by reading the document for you and returning exactly what you asked for — an answer, a summary, a translation, a plain-language explanation, or a set of practice questions — in seconds instead of minutes.

PDFly's AI tools are built around a simple privacy principle: your PDF stays with you. When you open a tool like Chat with PDF, the file is opened and its text is extracted locally, right there in your browser. Only the plain text needed to answer your specific request is sent to the AI model — never the original file — and that text isn't stored afterward or used to train anything. There's no account to create, no document library sitting on a server somewhere, and no watermark on the result.

This client-side-first approach matters for anything sensitive: employment contracts, medical reports, financial statements, or unpublished research. You get the convenience of an AI assistant without handing a permanent copy of a private document to a third party. It also means the tools are fast — there's no upload queue, and processing starts the moment your file is selected.

The benefits go beyond privacy and speed. AI PDF tools remove the manual, repetitive part of document work: skimming for a single fact, writing a summary for a colleague, translating a contract clause, or turning a chapter into a study guide. Students use them to unpack dense readings before an exam. Researchers use them to triage a stack of papers before deciding which ones deserve a full read. Professionals use them to get the gist of a report before a meeting, or to translate a document for an international client without waiting on a human translator.

Each tool below is purpose-built for a specific job rather than trying to do everything at once. Chat with PDF is best when you have specific questions and want direct, sourced answers. PDF Summarizer is best when you just need the main points quickly. PDF Translator is best when the barrier is language, not comprehension. Explain PDF is best for dense or technical material that needs breaking down into plain language. And the PDF Quiz Generator is best when you want to actively test what you've learned rather than passively re-read it. Together, they cover most of the ways people actually need to work with a PDF's content — not just its formatting.
