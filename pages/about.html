About PDFly

We build free, private PDF tools that work entirely in your browser. No servers that see your files. No accounts. No subscriptions. Just tools that work.

Privacy First

Your documents are personal. We engineered PDFly so that files are processed entirely in your browser — they never touch our servers.

Free Forever

No paywalls, no freemium tricks, no watermarks. PDFly is ad-supported to stay free. Every tool, unlimited use.

Blazing Fast

No upload round-trips. Processing happens locally at CPU speed. A 50MB PDF merges in seconds.

Works Everywhere

Fully responsive design works on desktop, tablet, and mobile. Light and dark modes. No app install needed.

Built With Open Source

PDFly is built entirely on open-source libraries that run in the browser. No proprietary black boxes.

pdf-lib

Create, modify, and read PDF documents in any JavaScript environment.

JSZip

Create, read and edit .zip files in JavaScript. Used for batch download.

mammoth.js

Convert .docx Word files to HTML/text. Powers Word to PDF conversion.
