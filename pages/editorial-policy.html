About Our Content

Editorial Policy

How we create and maintain the guides on our blog.

1. Our Purpose

The PDFly blog exists to help people get more out of our free PDF tools and to answer common questions about working with PDF, Word, Excel, and PowerPoint documents. Our goal is practical, accurate, and easy-to-follow guidance.

2. How We Write

Our articles are written and reviewed by our team to be clear, actionable, and free of unnecessary jargon. We aim to test the steps we describe using our own tools before publishing, and we prioritize accuracy over speed.

3. Keeping Content Current

Software features and best practices change over time. We periodically review older articles and update them when tools, terminology, or recommended workflows change.

4. Independence

Our editorial content is not influenced by advertisers. While our site may display ads to support free access to our tools, this has no bearing on which topics we cover or the recommendations we make.

5. Corrections

If you spot an error or outdated information in one of our articles, we want to know. Please reach out via our Contact page and we'll review and correct it promptly.

6. Related Policies

See also our Terms of Service and Disclaimer for information about how our content and tools may be used.
