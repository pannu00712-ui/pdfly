Security

How PDFly keeps your files safe.

1. Most Files Never Leave Your Device

PDFly's core tools run entirely in your web browser using JavaScript. When you use a file to merge, split, compress, or convert, that file is processed locally on your own computer — it is never transmitted to our servers, and we never see, store, or have access to its contents. Our AI-powered tools (Chat with PDF, Summarizer, Translator, Explain PDF, Generate Quiz, Rewrite PDF, and OCR) are the exception: they send extracted text to our server and AI provider to generate a result. See our Privacy Policy for exactly how that works.

2. No Server-Side Storage

For core tools, processing happens client-side, so there is no upload step and no server storage of your files at any point. Once you close or refresh the tab, any in-memory data from your session is cleared automatically. For AI-powered tools, extracted text passes through our server to reach our AI provider but is not stored by PDFly beyond completing your request.

3. Secure Connection

PDFly is served entirely over HTTPS, encrypting all traffic between your browser and our website's static assets (HTML, CSS, JavaScript). This protects against tampering or eavesdropping while the page loads.

4. Third-Party Libraries

Our tools use well-established open-source libraries (such as pdf-lib, pdf.js, and others) loaded from trusted content delivery networks to perform PDF operations directly in your browser. These libraries do not transmit your file data anywhere.

5. Password-Protected Files

When you use tools like Unlock PDF or Protect PDF, password verification and encryption/decryption happen locally in your browser. Your password is never sent over the network.

6. Reporting a Security Concern

If you believe you've found a security vulnerability in PDFly, please contact us at security@pdfly.uk. We take all reports seriously and will respond promptly.
