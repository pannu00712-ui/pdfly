Common Mistakes to Avoid

Forgetting to preview the final order before downloading — always do a final check of the thumbnail sequence.

Reordering a file you've already submitted somewhere — work on your local copy, not a version someone else already has.

Mixing up page numbers with thumbnail position — go by what's visually shown, not assumed page numbers.

FAQ — The Questions People Actually Have

Does reordering pages affect their content or quality?
No, only the page sequence changes — content stays exactly the same.

Can I organize a very long PDF, like 100+ pages?
Yes, though performance depends on your device's memory for very large files.

Can I undo a reorder before downloading?
Yes, you can keep rearranging until you're happy with the order, before hitting download.

Does this work with scanned PDFs?
Yes, works the same regardless of how the PDF was created.

What happens to my file after organizing?
Nothing is stored — once you close the tab, there's no copy anywhere except your download.
