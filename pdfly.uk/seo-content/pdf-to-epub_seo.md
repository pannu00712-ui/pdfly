## Why Convert PDF to EPUB?

Unlike PDFs, EPUB files use reflowable text that adapts to different screen sizes and font settings. Readers can increase text size, change fonts, and enjoy a more comfortable reading experience without constant zooming or horizontal scrolling.

Converting a PDF to EPUB is useful for ebooks, manuals, study materials, reports, novels, and other text-based documents that you want to read on an eReader or mobile device.

It's an easy way to make long documents more accessible and portable.

## Understanding the Limitations

Conversion quality depends on the structure of the original PDF. Text-based PDFs usually produce the best results.

Documents with complex layouts, multiple columns, tables, forms, or scanned pages may not convert perfectly because EPUB uses a flexible, reflowable layout rather than fixed pages.

Scanned PDFs may require OCR before conversion to create editable text.

## Tips for Better Results

Use PDFs with selectable text for more accurate conversions.

Review the EPUB after conversion to check headings, images, page breaks, and formatting, especially if the original document contains complex layouts.

For scanned documents, run OCR before converting to improve text quality.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### Which PDFs convert best to EPUB?

Text-based PDFs with simple layouts generally produce the best results. Scanned documents and complex page designs may require additional adjustments after conversion.

### Will the formatting stay exactly the same?

Not always. EPUB uses reflowable text, so the appearance may change to improve readability on different screen sizes.

### Can I convert scanned PDFs?

Yes, but scanned PDFs usually need OCR first so the text can be recognized before creating a useful EPUB file.

### Does the original PDF change?

No. Your original PDF remains untouched. PDFly creates a separate EPUB file while preserving the source document.

### Can I open the EPUB on any device?

EPUB is supported by many eReaders, reading apps, tablets, and smartphones. Some devices may require a compatible EPUB reader.

### Can I convert password-protected PDFs?

PDFs with reading restrictions may need to be unlocked before conversion.
