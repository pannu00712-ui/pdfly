## Understanding the Limitations

Editing capabilities depend on how the original PDF was created. Some scanned PDFs may require OCR before text can be edited, while password-protected PDFs with editing restrictions may need to be unlocked first.

Complex layouts, embedded fonts, or interactive elements may have editing limitations compared with standard text-based PDFs.

## Tips for Better Results

Save an original copy of your PDF before making major edits.

Review every page after editing to ensure formatting, spacing, and annotations appear correctly.

If you're working with a scanned document, use OCR first to improve text editing accuracy.

After completing your edits, consider flattening the PDF if you want annotations and form entries to become permanent.
