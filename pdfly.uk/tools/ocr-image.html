How OCR Image works

This tool uses AI to read text out of a photo, screenshot, or scanned image — handwriting, receipts, signs, whiteboard photos, or any picture with text in it. Since AI-based text recognition needs more processing power than a browser can provide, your image is securely sent to PDFly's OCR service for analysis and the extracted text is returned to you.

Images are processed only to extract the text you asked for, sent over an encrypted connection, and not used for anything else. There's no account required and nothing is watermarked.

Best Practices & Limitations

Tips for best results

  • Use a clear, well-lit, straight-on photo of the text for the most accurate results — angled or blurry shots reduce accuracy.
  • Higher resolution images generally produce more accurate text recognition than low-res ones.
  • Review the extracted text for errors, especially with handwriting, unusual fonts, or low-contrast images.

Limitations & when not to use this tool

  • Accuracy drops significantly on handwritten text, stylized fonts, or very low-resolution images.
  • Works best with a single dominant language per image — mixed-language text can reduce accuracy.
  • Not a substitute for proofreading when the extracted text will be used somewhere accuracy-critical.

Frequently asked questions

Is my image uploaded anywhere?

Yes. Unlike PDFly's browser-based PDF tools, OCR requires AI processing, so your image is sent securely to our OCR service, processed, and the result is returned to you.

Does it work on handwriting?

It can read clear handwriting reasonably well, though accuracy depends on legibility, image quality, and lighting — the same as any OCR tool.

Which image formats are supported?

JPG, PNG, and WebP images work best. Very low-resolution or blurry photos may produce inaccurate results.

Do I need to install anything?

No. Everything happens through your browser — no software, no account, no sign-up.
