How to Convert PDF to PNG: Lossless Images for Documents

By Alex Rivera · Updated July 2026 · 6 min read

You've got a PDF with fine text, crisp diagrams, and detailed charts. You need to turn it into images — but JPG compression would blur the text. You need PNG: lossless, sharp, and perfect for documents.

Why PNG instead of JPG?

  • Text-heavy documents: PNG keeps text edges sharp.
  • Diagrams and charts: Lines and shapes stay crisp.
  • Transparency: PNG supports transparent backgrounds.
  • Archiving: PNG is lossless, so you never lose quality.

Use cases for PDF to PNG

  • Image editing: PNGs are easier to edit in Photoshop or GIMP.
  • Transparency overlay: If you need to overlay images on other backgrounds.

Frequently Asked Questions

What's the difference between PDF to PNG and PDF to JPG?

PNG is lossless (no quality loss) but larger files. JPG is compressed (some quality loss) but smaller files.

Can I convert just one page to PNG?

Yes — most tools let you select a specific page or range of pages to convert.

Does PNG support transparency in PDFs?

PNG supports transparency, but it depends on the PDF. If the PDF has transparent elements, PNG can preserve them.
