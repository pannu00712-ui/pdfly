Each PDF page becomes a static PNG image after conversion. Selectable text, hyperlinks, form fields, and other interactive PDF features won't remain editable.

Large PDFs or pages with high-resolution graphics may produce larger PNG files than compressed image formats. Password-protected PDFs with viewing restrictions may need to be unlocked before conversion.

## Tips for Better Results

Choose a higher output resolution if your PDF contains detailed graphics, small text, or illustrations.
