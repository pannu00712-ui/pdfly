Common Mistakes to Avoid

Not double-checking page order before removing — thumbnails can shift after upload; confirm the right page is selected before deleting.

Removing pages from the original instead of a copy — keep a backup if you might need the removed pages later.

Assuming removed pages can be recovered — once downloaded, the original page is gone from that file; keep your source PDF safe.

FAQ — The Questions People Actually Have

Will removing pages affect the quality of the remaining pages?
No. Pages are removed at file level — no re-rendering or compression happens to what stays.

Can I remove multiple pages at once?
Yes, select as many as you want in one go and remove them together.

Does this work on scanned PDFs?
Yes, scanned and text-based PDFs both work the same way.

Is there a limit to how many pages I can remove?
No hard limit — you can even remove all but one page if needed.

What happens to the removed pages?
They're simply excluded from the new file. Nothing is stored or sent anywhere.
