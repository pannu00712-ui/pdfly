Common Mistakes to Avoid

Rotating all pages when only one is wrong — check each page first to avoid flipping correctly-oriented ones.

Confusing 90° clockwise with counter-clockwise — preview before downloading to confirm direction.

Re-scanning instead of just rotating — saves time to fix orientation digitally rather than redoing the scan.
