FAQ

Does this work on scanned PDFs?
Best results on digital PDFs; scanned ones may need OCR first.

Will formulas be preserved?
No, only the static values/text are extracted, not formulas.

Can I extract multiple tables from one PDF?
Yes, each table can usually be extracted into separate sheets.

Is there a page limit?
No hard limit, larger documents take longer to process.

Does formatting like bold/colors carry over?
Basic formatting only; complex styling may not transfer fully.
