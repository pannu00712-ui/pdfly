FAQ

Yes, for digital PDFs; scanned ones need OCR first.

Does formatting transfer exactly?
Mostly, but some manual adjustment is often needed.

Can I convert just a few pages?
Yes, select specific pages instead of the full document.

Is there a page limit?
No hard limit, larger files take longer to process.

Does this preserve images?
Yes, images are carried over into the slides.
