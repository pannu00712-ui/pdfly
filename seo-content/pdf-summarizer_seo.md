## Understanding the Limitations

Summary quality depends on the readability and structure of the PDF. Documents with searchable text generally produce better results than scanned image-only PDFs.

Scanned documents may require OCR before they can be summarized effectively. Highly technical content, complex tables, mathematical formulas, and diagrams may not always be reflected accurately in the summary.

Password-protected PDFs with reading restrictions may need to be unlocked before processing.

## Tips for Better Results

Use PDFs with clear headings and searchable text for the most accurate summaries.

Review the generated summary alongside the original document, especially when working with legal, financial, medical, or technical information.

If your PDF contains scanned pages, run OCR first to improve text recognition before summarizing.
