## Why Use OCR on a PDF?

Many PDFs are created by scanning paper documents rather than exporting digital files. Although they look like normal PDFs, each page is actually an image, making it impossible to search for words, copy text, or use many PDF editing tools.

OCR converts printed text within those scanned pages into machine-readable text, making the document much easier to work with. It's useful for digitizing paper records, searching archived documents, extracting information, and preparing PDFs for editing or accessibility.

Instead of manually retyping entire documents, OCR can save hours of work.

## Understanding the Limitations

OCR works best on clear scanned documents with high-resolution printed text.

Blurry scans, handwritten notes, faded pages, unusual fonts, or damaged documents may reduce recognition accuracy and require manual corrections.

Password-protected PDFs with reading or editing restrictions may need to be unlocked before OCR can be performed.

## Tips for Better Results

Use high-quality scans whenever possible to improve recognition accuracy.

If your document contains multiple languages, review the extracted text carefully to correct any recognition errors.

After OCR, search for a few keywords in the new PDF to confirm the text has been recognized successfully before sharing or editing the document.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### Which PDFs work best with OCR?

Scanned PDFs containing clear, printed text produce the most accurate results. Digital PDFs that already contain selectable text usually don't require OCR.

### Can OCR recognize handwritten text?

OCR is designed primarily for printed text. Handwritten notes may be recognized in some cases, but accuracy is generally lower.

### Will OCR change the appearance of my PDF?

The goal is to preserve the original layout while adding searchable text. Minor differences may occur depending on the quality and structure of the scanned document.

### Can I edit the recognized text afterward?

Yes. Once the PDF contains searchable text, it can usually be copied or edited using compatible PDF editing tools.

### Does OCR work on password-protected PDFs?

PDFs with security restrictions may need to be unlocked before OCR can be performed.

### Will my original PDF be modified?

No. Your original file remains unchanged. PDFly creates a separate OCR-enabled PDF while preserving the source document.
