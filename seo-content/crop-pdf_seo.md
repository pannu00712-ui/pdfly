Common Mistakes to Avoid

Cropping too tight and cutting off content — preview each page before finalizing, especially with text near edges.

Applying the same crop to all pages when sizes vary — scanned documents often have slightly different margins per page.

Cropping instead of rotating when the real issue is orientation — check if the page just needs rotating first.

FAQ — The Questions People Actually Have

Does cropping delete content permanently from the page?
Yes, cropped areas are removed from the visible page — keep your original file if you might need that content later.

Can I crop just specific pages, not the whole document?
Yes, apply different crop areas to individual pages as needed.

Will cropping reduce file size?
Slightly, since less content is fitted per page, but it's not a substitute for full compression.

Does this work on scanned and digital PDFs equally?
Yes, cropping works the same regardless of how the PDF was created.

Is there a limit to how much I can crop?
No artificial limit — you can crop down to any area size you select.
