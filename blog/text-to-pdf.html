How to Convert Text to PDF: Turn Plain Text Into Professional Documents

By Alex Rivera · Updated July 2026 · 4 min read

You've got a plain text file — maybe from a terminal output, a log file, or a simple note. But you need to share it as a PDF, so it looks professional and can't be easily edited.

Text to PDF is the simplest conversion there is. It takes your plain text and turns it into a formatted PDF document. You can even customize fonts, sizes, and page layout to make it look like a proper document.

When to convert text to PDF

  • Sharing: Turn plain text notes into shareable documents.
  • Professional presentation: Make text files look polished and official.
  • Archiving: Save text in a stable, widely readable format.
  • Printing: Convert text to PDF for easier printing with proper margins.

The fastest way to convert text to PDF

You don't need to paste into Word and export. A dedicated tool does it in seconds.

  1. Open a tool like PDFly's Text to PDF.
  2. Upload your .txt file, or paste your text directly into the tool.
  3. Choose your settings — font, size, page size, and margins.
  4. Click process and download your PDF.

What you can customize

  • Font: Choose from standard fonts like Arial, Times New Roman, or Courier.
  • Font size: Adjust the size for readability.
  • Page size: A4, Letter, Legal, or custom sizes.
  • Margins: Control the space around the text.
  • Orientation: Portrait or landscape.

Text to PDF vs. Word to PDF

  • Text to PDF: Takes plain text and formats it into a PDF. Simple, clean, minimal.
  • Word to PDF: Takes a formatted Word document and preserves that formatting. More complex.

Use Text to PDF when you have simple text and want a clean, readable document. Use Word to PDF when you need to preserve complex formatting.

Frequently Asked Questions

Can I add formatting to my text before converting?

Text files are plain — they don't support formatting. For bold, italic, or headings, you'd need to use a Word document instead.

Does Text to PDF support long documents?

Yes — the tool can handle large text files and will create multi-page PDFs as needed.

Can I convert text from the clipboard to PDF?

Yes — many tools let you paste text directly, so you don't need to save a file first.
