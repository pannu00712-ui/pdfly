How to Convert PDF to PowerPoint: Turn Documents Into Slides

By Alex Rivera · Updated July 2026 · 4 min read

You've got a report in PDF format, and you need to present it at a meeting. You could share the PDF, but it's not designed for presentations. You could copy and paste the content into PowerPoint — but that takes hours and you'll lose the formatting.

Or you could just convert the PDF to PowerPoint. Each page becomes a slide, with text that you can edit, images you can move, and formatting you can adjust.

Why PDF to PowerPoint?

Converting a PDF to PowerPoint gives you the best of both worlds: the fixed layout of the PDF becomes an editable slide deck. You can:

  • Edit text: Change wording, update numbers, fix typos.
  • Move elements: Reposition images, text boxes, and charts.
  • Add animations: Bring the content to life in your presentation.
  • Customize: Adjust colors, fonts, and layouts for your audience.

The fastest way to convert PDF to PowerPoint

You don't need to open Acrobat or use the built-in PowerPoint import. A browser-based tool does it in seconds.

  1. Open a tool like PDFly's PDF to PowerPoint.
  2. Upload your PDF.
  3. Wait a few seconds while the tool converts each page.
  4. Download your .pptx file and open it in PowerPoint.

That's it. Each page becomes a slide, and the content is editable.

What to expect from the conversion

The conversion aims to preserve as much of the original layout as possible. What transfers:

  • Text — it becomes editable text boxes in PowerPoint.
  • Images — they remain as images you can resize or move.
  • Tables — tables become tables in PowerPoint, though complex ones may need adjustments.
  • Layout — position of elements is generally preserved.

What might need adjustment:

  • Fonts — if the PDF uses a font you don't have, PowerPoint will substitute it.
  • Text boxes — some text might be split into multiple boxes.
  • Shapes — complex shapes might become images rather than editable shapes.

When to use PDF to PowerPoint

  • Business reports: Turn quarterly reports into presentations.
  • Academic papers: Convert research papers into lecture slides.
  • Proposals: Turn proposal documents into pitch decks.
  • Training materials: Convert training manuals into slide decks.

Frequently Asked Questions

Will my PDF text be editable in PowerPoint?

Yes — text from the PDF is extracted and turned into editable text boxes in PowerPoint. You can change, add, or delete text as needed.

Can I convert a scanned PDF to PowerPoint?

You'll need OCR first to extract text from the scanned pages. Some PDF-to-PPT tools include OCR; others require a separate step.

Will images from the PDF appear in PowerPoint?

Yes — images are preserved and can be moved, resized, or edited in PowerPoint.
