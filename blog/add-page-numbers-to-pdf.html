How to Add Page Numbers to a PDF (Without Breaking Your Flow)

By Alex Rivera · Updated July 2026 · 4 min read

You've just finished a 50-page report. It's perfect — except for one thing: there are no page numbers. Now you're manually typing "Page 1 of 50" on every single page, and you're already regretting it.

Page numbers seem like a small thing, but they're essential for contracts, reports, and any document that gets printed or shared. Without them, you end up with confused readers, misplaced sections, and that awkward moment when someone says "see page 27" and you have to count.

Adding page numbers to a PDF doesn't have to be tedious. In fact, it takes about 10 seconds with the right tool. Here's how.

Why PDFs don't show page numbers by default

PDFs display a page count in the viewer — "Page 1 of 50" — but that's part of the viewer interface, not the document itself. When you print the PDF, those numbers don't show up. And when someone opens it in a different reader, they might not even see the same count.

That's why you need to stamp page numbers directly onto the pages. It's not just about making the document look professional — it's about making it navigable.

The fastest way to add page numbers

You don't need to open Word, recreate the entire document, and then convert it back to PDF. You can add page numbers directly to any PDF using a browser-based tool.

  1. Open a tool like PDFly's Add Page Numbers.
  2. Upload your PDF.
  3. Choose the position for your numbers — bottom center, bottom right, top left, etc.
  4. Select a format: "Page 1," "1 of 10," or just "1."
  5. Click process and download your numbered PDF.

That's it. The tool handles the rest — adding the numbers to every page, starting from the first page or skipping the cover page if you prefer.

What about starting from a specific page?

Sometimes you want the first page to be unnumbered, with numbering starting from page 2. Most tools let you set a "start page" or "skip first page" option. For example, you might want page 2 to show as "Page 1" because the cover doesn't count.

PDFly's page number tool gives you that control. You can choose the starting page, the starting number, and whether to include or exclude the cover.

Keeping your document private

Like all PDFly tools, the page number tool works entirely in your browser. Your PDF never gets uploaded to a server. It stays on your device, and you download the result directly. That matters when you're working with sensitive documents like contracts, financial reports, or legal filings.

Frequently Asked Questions

Can I start numbering from a specific page?

Yes — most tools let you choose the starting page and starting number, so you can skip the cover and begin numbering from page 2.

Does adding page numbers affect the document quality?

No. The tool overlays numbers without compressing or degrading the original content. Quality stays intact.

Can I add page numbers to a scanned PDF?

Yes — even if the PDF is a scan, the page number tool can overlay numbers on the image-based pages.
