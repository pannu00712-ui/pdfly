How to Repair a Corrupted PDF: Fix Damaged Files

By Alex Rivera · Updated July 2026 · 4 min read

You've got a PDF that won't open. You click it, and your PDF reader says "this file is corrupted" or "can't read this file." It's a document you need — a contract, a report, something important. You could give up and ask for a new copy, but you need it right now.

Corrupted PDFs can often be repaired. The file structure may be damaged, but the content is still there. A repair tool can fix the structure and recover the readable content.

Why PDFs become corrupted

PDF corruption can happen for several reasons:

  • Incomplete downloads: The download stopped before the file was fully transferred.
  • Transfer errors: File transfer between devices or over networks can corrupt data.
  • Storage issues: Bad sectors on a hard drive or USB drive can damage files.
  • Software errors: The software that created or saved the PDF might have crashed.
  • Virus or malware: Malicious software can corrupt files.

The fastest way to repair a PDF

You don't need to use special recovery software. A browser-based repair tool can fix many corrupted PDFs.

  1. Open a tool like PDFly's Repair PDF.
  2. Upload the corrupted PDF.
  3. Click process — the tool attempts to repair the file.
  4. Download the repaired PDF and try opening it.

What the repair tool does

The tool works by:

  • Identifying structural errors: Finding broken references or corrupted metadata.
  • Rebuilding the structure: Recreating the file structure so it can be read.
  • Recovering readable content: Extracting text and images from the corrupted file.
  • Creating a new file: Outputting a new PDF that should be readable.

What can be recovered

  • Text: Most or all of the text from the document.
  • Images: Depending on the damage, images can often be recovered.
  • Layout: The structure of the pages may be recoverable.

What might not be recoverable:

  • Interactive elements: Forms, links, and bookmarks may be lost.
  • Perfect formatting: The repaired PDF may have minor formatting issues.

Frequently Asked Questions

Can every corrupted PDF be repaired?

Not always. If the file is severely damaged or the content is missing, repair may not be possible. But many common corruption issues can be fixed.

Does repair work with encrypted or password-protected PDFs?

You'll need to unlock the PDF first. If the encryption is corrupted, it may not be repairable.

Will I lose content when I repair a PDF?

The goal is to recover content. However, if part of the file is unrecoverable, that content may be missing from the repaired version.
