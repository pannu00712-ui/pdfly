How to Convert Images — PNG to JPG, WebP & More

By Alex Rivera · Updated July 2026 · 5 min read

You've got an image in the wrong format. PNG is too large, JPG doesn't support transparency, WebP is not supported everywhere. Converting between formats is essential for web, design, and sharing.

When to convert images

  • Web use: Convert to WebP for modern websites, JPG for compatibility.
  • Design: Convert to PNG for transparency, JPG for photos.
  • Email: Convert to JPG for smaller files.
  • Print: Convert to PNG for lossless quality.

Image formats compared

  • PNG: Lossless, supports transparency, larger files.
  • JPG: Lossy compression, no transparency, smaller files.
  • WebP: Modern format, supports both lossy and lossless, good compression.

Frequently Asked Questions

Does converting images lose quality?

Converting to PNG preserves quality. Converting to JPG loses some quality but reduces file size.

Can I convert multiple images at once?

Yes — upload multiple images and download them as a ZIP.

What's the best format for the web?

WebP is best for modern browsers. JPG is more widely compatible.
