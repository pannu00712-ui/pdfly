PDF Translator: Turn Documents Into Multiple Languages Instantly

By Alex Rivera · Updated July 2026 · 4 min read

You've got a contract in Spanish. Your client only speaks English. You could copy and paste the text into Google Translate — but you'd lose the formatting, and you'd have to do it page by page. Or you could just translate the entire PDF at once.

PDF Translator uses AI to translate your entire document into another language while preserving the original layout, fonts, and images. It's like having a professional translator on demand.

How it works

The tool extracts the text from your PDF, translates it using AI, and then rebuilds the document with the translated text in place.

  • Upload: You upload your PDF.
  • Select language: Choose the language you want to translate to.
  • Translate: The AI translates the entire document.
  • Download: You get a translated PDF with the same layout.

Languages supported

PDF Translator supports most major languages, including:

  • Popular languages: English, Spanish, French, German, Chinese, Japanese, Portuguese, Russian, Italian.
  • Regional languages: Arabic, Hindi, Korean, Turkish, Dutch, Polish, and many more.
  • Total: Over 50 languages supported.

What's preserved in translation

  • Formatting: Fonts, sizes, and styles are maintained.
  • Layout: Pages, margins, and positioning are preserved.
  • Images: Images remain in place with translated captions.
  • Tables: Table structure is preserved with translated content.

Frequently Asked Questions

How accurate is the translation?

The AI provides high-quality translation, but it may not be perfect for technical or highly specialized content. For critical documents, consider a human review.

Can I translate a scanned PDF?

Yes — if the tool includes OCR, it can extract text from scans and translate it, though accuracy depends on the scan quality.

Will the translated PDF look like the original?

Yes — the layout, fonts, and formatting are preserved. The text is translated, but the document looks the same.
