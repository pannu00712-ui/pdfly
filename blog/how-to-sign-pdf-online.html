How to Sign a PDF: Add Your Signature Without Printing or Scanning

By Alex Rivera · Updated July 2026 · 6 min read

You've got a contract to sign. You could print it, sign it with a pen, scan it, and email it back. That takes at least ten minutes and requires a printer and scanner. Or you could just sign it on your computer in thirty seconds.

Ways to create a signature

  • Draw: Use your mouse or touchpad to draw your signature. This is the fastest method.
  • Type: Type your name and choose a handwriting-style font. This looks clean and professional.
  • Upload: Upload an image of your signature from your computer. This looks the most like your physical signature.

How to sign a PDF

  1. Open PDFly's Sign PDF tool.
  2. Upload your PDF.
  3. Create your signature by drawing, typing, or uploading.
  4. Click on the page where you want to place your signature.
  5. Download your signed PDF.

Is a digital signature legally valid?

Yes — in most countries, a digital signature is legally binding. The key is that you intentionally added it to the document. For extra legal weight, some documents require a cryptographic digital signature, which verifies the identity of the signer.
