Chat with PDF: Ask Questions and Get Answers From Your Documents

By Alex Rivera · Updated July 2026 · 4 min read

You've got a 100-page PDF. You need to find one specific piece of information — a date, a name, a policy detail. You could scroll through every page. Or you could just ask.

Chat with PDF is an AI-powered tool that lets you ask questions about your PDFs. It reads your document, understands the content, and gives you direct answers. It's like having a conversation with your file.

How it works

The tool uses AI to analyze your PDF and create a searchable understanding of its content. When you ask a question, it looks for the answer and provides it with a reference to the source page.

  • Upload: You upload your PDF.
  • Analyze: The AI reads and indexes the document.
  • Ask: Type any question about the document.
  • Answer: The AI provides a direct answer with context.

What you can ask

  • Specific facts: "What's the deadline in section 3?"
  • Summaries: "Give me a summary of the main findings."
  • Comparisons: "How does this year's numbers compare to last year?"
  • Lists: "List all the action items in this document."

Frequently Asked Questions

Does Chat with PDF work with scanned PDFs?

The tool includes OCR, so it can read text from scanned documents and answer questions about it.

Is my PDF data private when I chat with it?

PDFly processes files locally in your browser. Your document is never uploaded to any server.

How accurate are the answers?

The AI is highly accurate for factual information found directly in your document. It provides references so you can verify the answer.
